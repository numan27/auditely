# Landing page mockup (approved)

The approved mockup is version 8 of the Design canvas "Auditely landing page"
(claude.ai/artifact/6AeB1dtDRubPAc3kvBvZ9v), with four artboards: Main, Running,
Result and Messages. The owner approved it without changes, so there are no
reactions to carry. Where the plan's Task 9 and this file differ, this file wins.

Breakpoints: 960px (the columns stack) and 600px (the header drops its section
links, the field and button stack). Light and dark both follow the app's theme.

## Main: the page, in order

### 1. Header

The logo and "Auditely" (to `/`), "Sample report" (`/#sample`) and "Pricing"
(`/#pricing`), then "Sign in" and the button "Start free", then the theme toggle.

### 2. Hero

- Headline: "Audit any website in about a minute."
- Lead: "Speed, SEO, mobile, SSL and AI-search readiness, scored and explained in
  plain English. Your first audit is free, with no sign-up."
- The field, labelled "Paste a website", placeholder "example.com", and the
  button "Audit it". One field, no illustration.
- Fine print: "One free audit per visitor. Sign up free to keep it, and run 5
  audits a month." (5 is the Free plan's audits.)

### 3. `#sample`: What you get back (a tinted band)

- Heading: "What you get back"
- Sub: "A real audit of a cosmetic dentist in Houston, exactly as Auditely wrote it."
- The report card:
  - "Cosmetic Dentists of Houston", the link houstondental.com, and the pill
    "Sample report";
  - seven score tiles: Performance 72, SEO 85, Mobile 37, SSL Valid, GEO 75,
    AEO 55, Lead capture 85;
  - "In plain English" and the summary, then "What to fix first" as a numbered
    list;
  - "Biggest issues on mobile";
  - the checks in three groups: "Visible to AI search (GEO)", "Ready for answer
    engines (AEO)" and "Can visitors reach you? (Lead capture)";
  - the card's footer: "See the sample search this came from", to `/demo`.

### 4. For agencies

- Heading: "Looking for clients? Audit a whole city."
- Sub: "Auditely finds the local businesses in any trade, audits every website,
  and writes each one a report worth sending."
- Three numbered steps:
  1. "Search a business type and a city" / "Every local business we can find,
     with its rating, reviews and website."
  2. "Audit every website" / "Seven scores for each site, and the problems worth
     calling about."
  3. "Send each one its report" / "A plain-English PDF and a first email, under
     your own name and logo on Pro and Agency."
- The link "See a sample search", to `/demo`.
- Beside them, a picture of Auditely's results for cosmetic dentists in Houston,
  TX, captioned "Business data from Google Maps".

### 5. `#pricing` (a tinted band)

- Heading: "Pricing"; sub: "Start free. Upgrade when you need more."
- The toggle: "Monthly" and "Yearly", with "two months free" beside it in green.
- Three cards:
  - Free, "For trying it out", $0, a quiet "Start free"; "1 search a month",
    "5 audits a month", "Plain-English summaries and PDF reports".
  - Pro, highlighted, "For freelancers", "$24 a month", "Choose Pro"; "25 searches
    a month", "250 audits a month", "100 email lookups a month", then "Audit a
    whole search at once", "Your name and logo on reports", "CSV and Google
    Sheets export", "Send from your mailbox", "HubSpot handoff".
  - Agency, "For agencies", "$59 a month", a quiet "Choose Agency"; "80 searches
    a month", "1,000 audits a month", "400 email lookups a month", "Everything in
    Pro".
- The note: "Prices are in US dollars, before sales tax. Yearly: Pro is $240 and
  Agency is $590. Cancel any time from Settings."

### 6. Closing

"Try it on a site you know." and the button "Audit a website" (back to the field,
`#site`).

### 7. Footer

The logo, Terms, Privacy, Contact, and "© {year} Auditely".

## Running

The hero keeps the headline (smaller) and the form, with the field read-only and
the button disabled, reading "Auditing". Below it, in the band, a card with a
spinner, "Auditing houstondental.com", "This takes about a minute. Keep this tab
open.", a progress bar, seven placeholder tiles (Performance, SEO, Mobile, SSL,
GEO, AEO, Lead capture) and placeholder lines.

## Result

The hero's form, then in the band:

- a dark band: "Keep this audit" and "Sign up free and it's saved to your account,
  with a PDF to send and 5 more audits every month.", with "Sign up free" and a
  quiet "Sign in";
- the report card: the host (houstondental.com) as its name, the full address as
  the link, "Audited just now" as plain text, the same scores, summary, fixes,
  issues and checks, and no footer;
- under it: "Your free audit is used. Sign up free to run 5 audits a month, or
  audit a whole city's businesses on Pro."

## Messages

Each shows under the form as a row with an icon:

- again: "You've had your free audit, so here's the one you ran. Sign up free to
  keep it and run 5 audits a month." with "Sign up free" (the result follows);
- visitor: "Someone on this network has already had the free audit. Sign up free
  to run 5 audits a month." with "Sign up free";
- cap: "Free audits are paused for today. Sign up free to run your own now, or
  try again tomorrow." with "Sign up free";
- failed: "Couldn't audit example.com: the site didn't respond. Check the address
  and try again. This didn't use your free audit." (a warning, no button);
- notPublic: "That isn't a public website. Enter the site's address, like
  example.com." (a warning, no button);
- bot: "Your browser didn't pass our automated check. Reload the page and try
  again." (a warning, no button).

Free audits off: in place of the form, a box reading "Sign up to audit a website."
with "Sign up free".
